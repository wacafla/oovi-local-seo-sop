Monthly reports are the primary way clients see the value of their investment. A clear, well-structured report that shows movement and explains what drove results builds trust and reduces churn.

## Report Components

Every monthly report must include the following six sections. Use the Oovi Monthly Report Template in Google Drive as your starting point — do not skip or abbreviate any section.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Rank Tracking Summary" icon="chart-line">
    Current rankings for all target keywords, including month-over-month movement. Highlight keywords that entered the top 3 or moved significantly in either direction.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GBP Insights" icon="map-pin">
    Search views, map views, clicks to website, calls, and direction requests pulled directly from the Google Business Profile dashboard for the reporting period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Website Traffic" icon="chart-bar">
    Organic sessions, new users, top landing pages, and any tracked conversion events from Google Analytics. Always filter to the organic channel only.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Citation & Review Progress" icon="star">
    Number of new citations built this month, new reviews received, and the client's current average star rating. Note any negative reviews that were responded to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Work Completed This Month" icon="check">
    A bullet list of everything completed during the reporting period: GBP posts published, links built, content published, technical fixes implemented, and any other deliverables.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Next Month's Plan" icon="arrow-right">
    The top three priorities for the coming month. Be specific — list the actual tasks planned, not generic statements like "continue link building."
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Report Preparation Process

Begin pulling data no later than the 28th of each month so you have time to complete the report, get it reviewed, and send it on schedule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pull rank tracking data from BrightLocal">
    Log in to BrightLocal, navigate to the client's campaign, and export the rank tracking report. Set the date range to the full calendar month. Verify that all target keywords are being tracked before exporting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export GBP Insights for the reporting period">
    Open the client's Google Business Profile dashboard and navigate to the Insights section. Export data for the same calendar month you are reporting on. Download or screenshot all relevant metrics: searches, views, clicks, calls, and direction requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull website traffic data from Google Analytics">
    In Google Analytics, filter by the organic channel and set the date range to match the reporting month. Record organic sessions, new users, top pages by traffic, and any conversion events. Compare the same period from the previous month to show movement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log citations and reviews">
    Check BrightLocal's citation tracker for the number of new citations built this month. Open the client's Google Business Profile and count new reviews received during the reporting period. Record the current average star rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the Work Completed section">
    Open ClickUp and filter tasks to the client's project for the reporting month. List every completed task as a bullet point in plain language — avoid internal jargon. This section should reflect exactly what the client paid for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Oovi Monthly Report Template">
    Open the client's report file in Google Drive (located in the client's folder under Reports). Populate all six sections with the data you have gathered. Ensure all charts and tables are updated and that the report date range is correct throughout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Have the Account Manager review the report">
    Share the completed report with the Account Manager via Google Drive and notify them in Slack. Do not send the report to the client until the Account Manager has reviewed and approved it. Allow at least 24 hours for the review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to the client by the 3rd of the following month">
    Once approved, send the report to the client by email. Include a short personal note summarizing the highlight of the month — do not just attach the report without context. Log the send date in ClickUp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Report Review Checklist

Before sending any report for Account Manager review, run through this checklist yourself.

<Note>
  Verify each of the following before marking the report as ready for review:

  - All data covers the correct calendar month — double-check date ranges in every section
  - Rank tracking includes enough keywords to tell a meaningful story (minimum 10 target keywords)
  - The report template uses the client's branding colors if they have been set up for white-label reporting
  - The "Next Month's Plan" section lists specific, actionable tasks — not vague statements
  - All charts and graphs are fully rendered and not showing placeholder or loading states
  - The client's name and business name are spelled correctly throughout
</Note>

<Tip>
  Add one to two sentences of plain-language explanation to any metric that shows a significant change — positive or negative. Clients want to know why something happened, not just that it did. A quick note like "Rankings improved this month after we resolved the duplicate listing issue" goes a long way toward building trust and demonstrating your expertise.
</Tip>
