On your first day, getting your tools set up correctly is the priority. Every platform we use serves a specific purpose in how we deliver campaigns, track results, and communicate with clients — and being locked out of the wrong thing at the wrong moment creates delays that ripple across accounts. Work through the setup checklist below in order, then familiarize yourself with the core tools reference so you know what each platform is for and how we expect it to be used.

## New Hire Setup Checklist

Complete these steps in order during your first day or first week. Your manager will assist with any steps that require admin approval.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Oovi Company Email">
    Receive your Oovi company email address from the Operations Manager and set a strong, unique password immediately. This email is your identity across all agency platforms — keep it secure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the Project Management Platform">
    Log in to ClickUp or Asana — your manager will send you an invite to your Oovi email. Explore your assigned workspace and make sure you can see the client projects relevant to your role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the Shared Google Drive">
    Accept the invite to the Oovi shared Google Drive folder. Your access will be scoped to the client accounts you're working on. Review the folder structure so you know where to find and save files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Google Search Console and Analytics Access">
    Ask your manager or the Reporting Analyst to add you as a user to the Google Search Console and Google Analytics properties for your assigned clients. Confirm you have read access at minimum before your first week is out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up BrightLocal or Whitespark">
    Log in to BrightLocal or Whitespark using your Oovi credentials (provided by the Operations Manager). Locate the projects for your assigned clients and review the existing rank tracking and citation audit data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Added to SEMrush or Ahrefs">
    The Operations Manager will add you to the shared agency seat in SEMrush or Ahrefs. Once you have access, do not run large-scale site crawls without checking in first — crawl credits are shared across the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Two-Factor Authentication">
    Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) on every platform before you begin client work. This is a non-negotiable security requirement. Use an authenticator app rather than SMS where the option is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Onboarding Walkthrough">
    Schedule and complete the onboarding walkthrough with your manager. This session covers your specific client accounts, your first deliverables, and any questions you have after working through this documentation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core Tools Reference

<Accordion title="Google Business Profile Manager">
  **What it's for:** Managing and optimizing client Google Business Profile listings — including business information, photos, posts, Q&A, and review responses.

  **How to access:** Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and log in using the client's designated Google account. Access is managed through delegated ownership — your Oovi email should be added as a manager on each listing you work on.

  **Important:** Always use the client's Google account with delegated access — never your personal Google account. Mixing personal and client accounts causes ownership conflicts that are difficult and time-consuming to resolve.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="BrightLocal">
  **What it's for:** Local rank tracking, citation audits, review monitoring, and local search reporting. BrightLocal is the primary platform for measuring the health and visibility of a client's local presence.

  **How to access:** Log in at [brightlocal.com](https://www.brightlocal.com) using your Oovi-provided credentials. Each client has a dedicated project — never mix data between client projects.

  **Key features you'll use:** Rank Tracker (monitor positions in local and maps results), Citation Tracker (find missing or inconsistent citations), and Review Monitoring (track new reviews across platforms).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="SEMrush / Ahrefs">
  **What it's for:** Keyword research, backlink analysis, competitor audits, and site health monitoring. Both tools offer overlapping functionality — confirm with the Operations Manager which one is your team's primary platform.

  **How to access:** You'll be added to the shared agency seat. Log in with the credentials provided. These seats have usage limits — be mindful of large crawl jobs and coordinate with the Operations Manager if you need to run an intensive audit.

  **Best practice:** Use templates and saved reports wherever possible to avoid redundant crawl usage across the team.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Google Search Console & Analytics">
  **What it's for:** Monitoring organic search performance (Search Console) and website traffic behavior (Analytics) for client properties. These are the ground-truth sources for reporting on clicks, impressions, rankings, and user engagement.

  **How to access:** Each client domain must be verified in Search Console. Request access to existing properties via the "Add Users" option in GSC settings — you'll need the property owner or an existing admin to approve you. For Analytics, access is granted through the GA4 property settings.

  **Note:** You should have at least Restricted (read-only) access for every client you contribute to. Full edit access is reserved for Account Managers and the Operations Manager.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="ClickUp / Asana">
  **What it's for:** All task and project management at Oovi runs through this platform. Every deliverable, deadline, and assignment is tracked here. If a task isn't logged, it doesn't exist from a accountability or billing standpoint.

  **How to access:** Your manager will invite you via your Oovi email during your first day setup. Once in, review the workspace structure and ask your manager to walk you through the conventions for task naming, due dates, and status updates.

  **Rule:** Never work from memory or a private to-do list for client deliverables. Log everything — completed tasks, blockers, and notes — so the whole team has visibility.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Google Drive">
  **What it's for:** Centralized storage for all client documentation, including audit files, report templates, strategy documents, and creative assets. Drive is the single source of truth for client files at Oovi.

  **How to access:** You'll receive a shared folder invite to your Oovi email. Folders are organized by client name. Within each client folder, you'll find subfolders for audits, reports, content, and correspondence.

  **Naming convention:** Follow the established file naming format for your team — ask your manager for the current convention if it isn't immediately obvious from the existing files.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Slack / Team Messaging">
  **What it's for:** Day-to-day internal communication. Slack (or the team's preferred messaging platform) is for quick questions, status updates, and flagging time-sensitive issues — not for assigning tasks or making decisions that need a record.

  **Key channels:**
  - **#general** — Company-wide updates and announcements
  - **#client-updates** — Significant changes, wins, or issues on client accounts
  - **#seo-team** — SEO-specific discussion, industry news, and team questions

  **Best practice:** If a conversation in Slack leads to an action item, move it into the project management tool immediately so it doesn't get lost.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  Never use a client's login credentials directly. All client accounts — including Google Business Profile, Google Analytics, and any third-party platforms — must be accessed through the agency's email with properly granted delegated access. Logging in as the client creates security risks and can complicate account recovery if access is ever disputed.
</Warning>
