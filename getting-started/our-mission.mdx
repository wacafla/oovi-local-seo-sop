## What We Do

Oovi Local SEO delivers a focused set of services designed specifically for local businesses competing in geographically defined markets. Our core service offering includes Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization, citation building and cleanup, on-page SEO for local landing pages, link building with a local and niche relevance focus, and review management strategy. Each of these disciplines plays a distinct role in how Google evaluates and ranks a local business — and together, they form the foundation of every campaign we run.

## Why Local SEO Matters

Local SEO is not simply a smaller version of general SEO. It operates on a different set of signals and serves a fundamentally different user intent. When someone searches for "plumber near me" or "best pizza in [city]," Google's algorithm weighs three distinct factors: **proximity** (how close the business is to the searcher), **relevance** (how well the business matches the search query), and **prominence** (how well-known and trusted the business appears based on links, citations, reviews, and engagement). Mastering those three signals — not just keyword density or backlink count — is what separates effective local SEO from the generic approach, and it's where Oovi focuses its expertise.
