A smooth onboarding experience sets the tone for the entire client relationship. At Oovi, onboarding follows a consistent, step-by-step process so nothing is missed and clients feel confident from day one. Every role on the team has a defined part to play — and when each person executes their piece on time, the transition from "new client" to "active campaign" feels seamless for everyone involved.

## Onboarding Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contract Signed">
    The Account Manager confirms scope, deliverables, and start date with the client. A new project is created in ClickUp and all relevant team members are added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Intake Form Sent">
    The Account Manager sends the standard Oovi intake form to the client. The client is expected to complete it within 48 hours of signing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initial Audit">
    The SEO Specialist runs a full local SEO audit within 5 business days of receiving the completed intake form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kickoff Call">
    The Account Manager leads a 45-minute onboarding call with the client to walk through audit findings, align on goals, and outline the first 30 days of the campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Campaign Launch">
    The SEO Specialist begins active optimization work based on audit findings and the priorities established during the kickoff call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who Does What

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Account Manager" icon="user-tie">
    Owns all client communication and timeline management throughout the onboarding process. Leads the kickoff call and ensures every stage transitions on schedule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SEO Specialist" icon="magnifying-glass-chart">
    Runs the initial local SEO audit, requests and confirms GBP access, and executes the first round of optimization tasks following the kickoff call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content Writer" icon="pen-nib">
    Drafts the client welcome email (when applicable) and handles initial GMB post setup as part of early campaign deliverables.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reporting Analyst" icon="chart-bar">
    Creates the client's project in BrightLocal and establishes baseline rank tracking so performance can be measured from day one.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  All onboarding tasks must be logged in ClickUp. If a step is delayed for any reason, flag it in the **#client-updates** Slack channel immediately so the team can adjust accordingly.
</Note>
