Before you can start optimizing, you need accurate information from the client. Use this checklist every time — missing items early causes delays later. The intake form is your single source of truth for everything about the client's business, their assets, and what success looks like to them. Treat it as a prerequisite, not a formality.

## Intake Form Sections

<Accordion title="Business Information">
  Collect the following core business details — accuracy here directly affects NAP consistency across all citations and listings:

  - **Business name** — exact legal name as it appears on official documents
  - **Primary address** — full street address including suite/unit number if applicable
  - **Service areas** — all cities, zip codes, or regions they serve
  - **Phone number** — primary business number (must match GBP listing)
  - **Website URL** — the domain they want to rank
  - **Business category** — primary GBP category plus any relevant secondary categories
  - **Hours of operation** — regular hours and any special/holiday hours
  - **Year established** — used for citation profiles and trust signals
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Google Assets">
  You need access to all relevant Google properties before the audit can begin:

  - **Gmail / Google account email** — the address associated with their Google Business Profile
  - **Google Analytics** — request access at the Admin level
  - **Google Search Console** — request access as Full User
  - **Google Ads account** — collect account ID if applicable; note whether campaigns are currently active
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Existing Profiles">
  Document all known listing and directory presence to inform the citation audit:

  - **Current GBP URL** — direct link to their Google Business Profile
  - **Yelp listing URL** — if they have a claimed profile
  - **Facebook page URL** — business page, not a personal profile
  - **Other directories** — any additional platforms the client knows about (e.g., Angi, Houzz, Healthgrades, Avvo)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Competitor Info">
  Understanding who they're competing against shapes keyword research and the audit benchmarks:

  - **2–3 named competitors** — businesses they consider direct local competitors
  - **Priority targets** — any competitors they specifically want to outrank, and why
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Goals & KPIs">
  Clarify what success looks like before a single optimization task is assigned:

  - **Primary goal** — rankings, inbound calls, website traffic, form fills, or a combination
  - **Current monthly leads estimate** — how many leads they currently receive per month
  - **Priority services** — the top 2–3 services they most want to rank for
  - **Priority locations** — the cities or neighborhoods that matter most to their business
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Content & Brand">
  Gather brand assets needed for content creation and GMB posts:

  - **Brand logo** — high-resolution file (PNG or SVG preferred)
  - **Brand colors** — hex codes if known
  - **Preferred brand voice** — professional, friendly, authoritative, conversational, etc.
  - **Past content** — any existing blog posts, service pages, or marketing copy they want to preserve or build on
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Access Credentials">
  Website and platform login credentials must be collected and stored correctly:

  - **Website CMS login** — WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, or other platform credentials
  - **Storage rule** — all credentials go directly into the agency password manager. **Never store passwords in ClickUp, Slack, or email.**
</Accordion>

## Sending the Intake Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the Standard Template">
    Open the official Oovi intake form template in Google Drive. Do not create a one-off version — the template is the standard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Within 24 Hours">
    Share the form link with the client via email within 24 hours of the contract being signed. Include a brief note explaining what it's for and why it matters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow Up at 48 Hours">
    If the client hasn't completed the form within 48 hours, send a polite follow-up email. Flag the delay in the #client-updates Slack channel if it risks pushing back the audit start date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review for Completeness">
    Once the form is submitted, review it alongside the SEO Specialist before officially kicking off the audit. Identify any missing or unclear items and follow up with the client to resolve them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never begin any optimization work until the intake form is at least 80% complete. Missing business name, address, or GBP access will block the entire workflow and delay the audit, the kickoff call, and campaign launch.
</Warning>
